A B O U T   M E

My name is Kyla and I have a love for storytelling. I focus in visual development and storyboarding, but I also enjoy animating. You will often find me working on personal story projects and making cosplays or props in my free time.

Some series that have inspired me include Don't Hug Me I'm Scared, Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse, The Amazing World of Gumball, The Amazing Digital Circus, ENA Dream BBQ, Smile For Me, A Hat in Time, Popee the Performer, and Deltarune.

I am drawn towards wacky stories with bright colors and thought-provoking narratives. I hope to one day create something similar to the things that inspired me!
